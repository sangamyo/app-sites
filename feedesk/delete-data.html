Delete your FeeDesk data

FeeDesk has no accounts and no server that stores your data. Everything you enter is saved on your phone (and, if your phone’s Google backup is on, in your own Google account backup), so you delete it there.

Option 1: Delete all data in the app

  1. Open FeeDesk.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Under Your data, tap Delete all data.
  4. Tap Delete everything, then Yes, delete to confirm.

This immediately and permanently erases your business details, every plan, member or student, payment, receipt record and attendance check-in from the phone, and your settings, and cancels the morning summary notifications.

Option 2: Uninstall the app

Uninstalling FeeDesk removes the app and all of its data from your phone. If your phone’s Google backup is turned on, Android may keep a copy of app data in your Google account backup; you can manage or delete it in your phone’s settings under Google → Backup (the exact menu names vary by phone).

Backup files, receipts and messages

If you exported a backup file (for example to Google Drive, email or WhatsApp), delete that file there too. Receipts you shared and WhatsApp messages you sent stay wherever you sent them. We never receive any of these.

If you are a member or student

Your gym, studio or tutor keeps your details on their own phone; we cannot see or delete them. Please ask them directly to delete your details. They can delete one member or student in the app, or all data as above.

Subscription records

Deleting data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records linked to a random app user ID (not your name). To ask us to request deletion of the RevenueCat record, open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues titled “FeeDesk purchase record deletion”. Do not post your Google Play order number, email address or any other personal details there: issues are public. We will reply on the issue with a private way to send the order number (it starts with GPA.), then ask RevenueCat to delete the record, usually within 30 days.

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues and say which app: FeeDesk. Issues there are public, so please do not include personal details.

What is deleted: all app data on the phone, immediately. What may be kept: a copy in your own Google account backup until you delete it or it is replaced, any files or messages you shared yourself, and Google Play / RevenueCat purchase records, kept under their policies for tax, accounting and fraud prevention. We hold no other data about you.
