Privacy policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what information the FeeDesk Android app and this website (together, "the app") handle. The app is made by an independent developer, the developer named on FeeDesk’s Google Play listing ("we", "us").

The short version. FeeDesk has no accounts and no server of its own. Everything you enter, including your business details, members or students, fee plans, payments, receipts and attendance, is stored on your phone. We cannot see it. If your phone’s Google backup is turned on, Android may copy the app’s data into your own Google account backup, which Google stores and you control in your phone’s settings. The only other information that leaves your phone is subscription purchase data handled by Google Play and RevenueCat, plus anything you choose to share yourself. The app shows no ads, has no analytics or crash reporting, and we do not sell data.

1. Data stored on your phone

  • Your business: business name, business type (gym, studio or tuition class), currency, and your phone number and address if you add them for your receipts.
  • Plans and fee types: name, amount, period or class-pack size, and batch or time slot.
  • Members or students: name, phone number, plan, start date, batch, status (active, paused or left), notes and, for tuition classes, a guardian’s name.
  • Payments you record (amount, method such as cash, UPI, card or bank, and date), receipt numbers and attendance check-ins.
  • Your settings, such as the reminder language (English or Hindi), reminder message templates and your morning summary time.

This data is kept in a database inside the app’s private storage on your phone. It is never uploaded to us. If you uninstall the app or use Delete all data, it is erased (see Delete your data).

You are responsible for the members’ and students’ information you enter. Only record what you need to run your classes, and tell your members (or, for children, their parents or guardians) that you keep these records.

Android Auto Backup (your Google account)

FeeDesk keeps Android Auto Backup enabled. If backup is turned on in your phone’s settings, Android may copy the app’s data (including the database above) into your own Google account backup, stored by Google under Google’s privacy policy. We never receive or see this backup. When you set up a new phone with the same Google account and reinstall FeeDesk, Android can restore it. To turn this off, go to your phone’s Settings → Google → Backup (the exact menu names vary by phone) and switch off backup; you can also delete existing backups there.

2. Data that leaves your phone

Subscriptions (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription. RevenueCat and Google Play receive a random app user ID created on your phone (not your name, phone or email) and purchase information such as the product, price, purchase date, trial status and renewal status, plus basic device information (for example the app version, OS version and country/store). See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy.

WhatsApp reminders

When you tap to remind a member, FeeDesk opens WhatsApp (or your browser, if WhatsApp is not installed) with that member’s phone number and a prefilled message. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. FeeDesk never sends messages automatically. Messages you send are handled by WhatsApp under WhatsApp’s privacy policy.

Receipts, backups and other things you share

PDF receipts are created on your phone. When you share a receipt or export a backup file, the content goes wherever you choose to send it (for example WhatsApp, email or Google Drive), under that service’s own privacy policy. A backup file contains all of your FeeDesk data, including members’ names and phone numbers, in readable form. Store it somewhere safe.

Reminders to you

The morning summary of fees due today and overdue is a local notification scheduled on your phone. No push server is involved, and nothing is sent to your members. The app asks for notification permission once, after you add your first member or student. You can turn the summary off or change its time in Settings.

No other network use

Apart from RevenueCat and Google Play for subscriptions, FeeDesk does not send your data anywhere and has no analytics, crash reporting or advertising software. The links the app opens are WhatsApp reminders (described above), this privacy policy and terms of use, and Google Play’s subscription page; they open only when you tap them.

3. What we do not collect

  • No account, email address or password. There is no sign-in.
  • No bank logins, card numbers or UPI PINs. The app never collects or moves money.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, SMS, phone state, or photos and other files. Phone numbers are typed in by you. Restoring a backup uses the system file picker, which only gives the app the one file you pick. The app asks for permission to show notifications (for the morning summary); Google Play billing and the internet connection are used only for subscriptions.
  • No biometric data. Attendance is marked by tapping a name.
  • No advertising identifiers, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.

4. How long data is kept

Data on your phone stays until you delete it in the app, use Delete all data, or uninstall the app. A copy in your Google account backup is kept and deleted by Google according to your backup settings (see section 1). Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies (for example for tax, accounting and fraud prevention). You can ask us to request deletion of the RevenueCat record linked to your app user ID (see section 8).

5. Your choices and rights

You can view, edit, export and delete all of your data in the app at any time. Because we do not hold your data, we cannot view, correct or recover it for you, so please keep a backup. If one of your members asks you to delete their details, you can delete them in the app. Depending on where you live (for example India, the EU or California), you or your members may have additional rights under local law; contact us (see section 8) and we will help where we can.

6. Children

The app is meant for adults who run a gym, studio or tuition class. It is not intended for children to use. Tuition-class owners may record details about students who are children; please collect only what you need and follow the rules on children’s data that apply where you live.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and for important changes we will tell you in the app.

8. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues and say which app: FeeDesk. Issues there are public, so please do not include personal details such as your members’ names or phone numbers, your receipts, your email address or your Google Play order number. If we need something private from you (for example to find a RevenueCat purchase record), we will reply on the issue with a private way to send it.
