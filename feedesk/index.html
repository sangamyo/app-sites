Know who has paid, who is due, and who came today, in one tap.

For small gyms, yoga and dance studios, and tuition classes. No more registers: one screen shows fees collected, fees overdue and today’s attendance.

Get it on Google Play

Android, coming soon to Google Play. Free for up to 15 active members or students; the optional Pro subscription unlocks unlimited members, PDF receipts, bulk reminders and attendance reports. No ads, no account and no sign-up.

  • Paid, due soon, overdue

    Monthly, quarterly, yearly or class-pack plans. FeeDesk works out each next due date, including part payments.

  • Polite WhatsApp reminders

    One tap opens WhatsApp with a ready-made reminder in English or Hindi. You check it and press send. Nothing is sent automatically.

  • Attendance and receipts

    Tap to mark who came today, per batch. Record a payment (cash, UPI, card or bank) and share a numbered PDF receipt.

FeeDesk only keeps records. It never collects, holds or transfers fees or any other money. Members pay you the way they always do.

Your data stays on your phone

There is no account and no FeeDesk server. Members, payments and attendance are saved on your phone, and in your own Google account backup if your phone’s backup is on. Use Backup & restore in Settings to save a backup file wherever you like (for example Google Drive) and to move to a new phone.
