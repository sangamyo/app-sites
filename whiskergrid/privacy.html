Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Whisker Grid ("the app") is a single-player puzzle game. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. It does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data. It does not use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services, and it contains no in-app purchases.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress (solved levels, daily puzzle results, your streak, and your settings) in the app's private storage on your device only. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app, or using "Reset progress" in Settings, removes it.

Sharing

If you tap "Share result", the app opens your device's standard share sheet with a short text (your time and streak). Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose an app to share it with.

Internet access

The app works fully offline and does not connect to any server operated by us.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third-party services

The app is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Those platforms may collect information as described in their own privacy policies, for example when you download or update the app. We do not receive personal data from them.

Changes to this policy

If the app adds features that handle personal data, this policy will be updated before those features are released, and the date above will change.
