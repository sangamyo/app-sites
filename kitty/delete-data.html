Delete your Turnpot data

Turnpot has no accounts and no server that stores your data. Everything you enter is saved on your phone (and, if your phone’s Google backup is on, in your own Google account backup), so you delete it there.

Option 1: Delete all data in the app

  1. Open Turnpot.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Under Your data, tap Delete all data.
  4. Tap Delete everything, then Yes, delete to confirm.

This immediately and permanently erases every committee, member, payment, round result and draw record, your organiser details and your settings from the phone, and cancels scheduled reminders.

Option 2: Uninstall the app

Uninstalling Turnpot removes the app and all of its data from your phone.

Remove the copy in your Google account backup

If your phone’s Google backup is turned on, Android may keep a copy of Turnpot’s data in your own Google account backup, even after you delete data in the app or uninstall it, until that backup is replaced or deleted. To remove it, open your phone’s Settings → Google → Backup (the exact menu names vary by phone) and delete the backup or turn off backup, or manage backups from the Google Drive app (Backups). We never receive this backup and cannot delete it for you.

Backup files, statements and messages

If you exported a Turnpot backup file (for example to Google Drive, email or WhatsApp), delete that file there too. PDF statements, text summaries and reminder messages you shared stay wherever you sent them. We never receive any of these.

Subscription records

Deleting data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records linked to a random app user ID (not your name). To ask us to request deletion of the RevenueCat record, open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues titled “Turnpot subscription record deletion”. Do not post your Google Play order number, email address or any other personal details there. We will reply on the issue with a private way to send the order number (it starts with GPA.), then ask RevenueCat to delete the record, usually within 30 days.

If you are a committee member

Your organiser keeps your name, phone number and payment records on their own phone; we cannot see or delete them. Please ask your organiser directly.

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues and please say which app: Turnpot. Issues there are public, so please do not include personal details.

What is deleted: all app data on the phone, immediately. What may be kept: a copy in your own Google account backup until you delete it or it is replaced, any files or messages you shared yourself, and Google Play / RevenueCat purchase records, kept under their policies for tax, accounting and fraud prevention. We hold no other data about you.
