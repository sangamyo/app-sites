Privacy policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This policy explains what information the Turnpot Android app and this website (together, "the app") handle. The app is made by an independent developer, the developer named on Turnpot’s Google Play listing ("we", "us"). Turnpot is a record book for the organiser of a committee, kitty or BC savings circle.

The short version. Turnpot has no accounts and no server of its own. Everything you enter, including your committees, your members’ names and phone numbers, payments and draw results, is stored on your phone. We cannot see it. If your phone’s Google backup is turned on, Android may copy the app’s data into your own Google account backup (Android Auto Backup), which Google stores and you control in your phone’s settings. Subscription purchase data is handled by Google Play and RevenueCat. Messages, PDFs and backup files go out only when you choose to share them. The app shows no ads, has no analytics, and we do not sell data.

1. Data stored on your phone

  • Your organiser details (optional): your name and phone number, shown on the statements you create.
  • Committees: name, monthly amount per share, number of rounds, start month, collection day and payout method (lucky draw, fixed order or bid).
  • Members: names, optional phone numbers, number of shares and turn order. These are details of other people that you type in.
  • Payments you record (amount, date and an optional note) and round results (who received the pot, the bid discount if any, and the time of a lucky draw).
  • Your preferences: language, currency and whether collection-day reminders are on.

This data is kept in a database inside the app’s private storage on your phone. It is never uploaded to us. If you uninstall the app or use Delete all data, it is erased (see Delete your data).

Android Auto Backup (your Google account)

Turnpot keeps Android Auto Backup enabled so your committee records can survive a lost or replaced phone. If backup is turned on in your phone’s settings, Android may copy the app’s data (including the database above) into your own Google account backup, stored by Google under Google’s privacy policy. We never receive or see this backup. When you set up a new phone with the same Google account and reinstall Turnpot, Android can restore it. To turn this off, go to your phone’s Settings → Google → Backup (the exact menu names vary by phone) and switch off backup; you can also delete existing backups there.

Your members’ information

Members’ names and phone numbers are stored only on the organiser’s phone (and in the organiser’s own backups). Members do not use the app and we never receive their details. As the organiser, you are responsible for the members’ information you enter and share: only record what you need to run the committee, tell your members that you keep these records, and keep backup files and statements safe.

2. Data that leaves your phone

Subscriptions (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription. RevenueCat and Google Play receive a random app user ID created on your phone (not your name, phone or email) and purchase information such as the product, price, purchase date, trial status and renewal status, plus basic device information (for example the app version, OS version and country/store). No committee or member data is sent. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy.

Things you choose to share

When you send a reminder through WhatsApp, share a text summary or a PDF statement, or export a backup file, the content goes wherever you choose to send it (for example WhatsApp, email or Google Drive), under that service’s own privacy policy. Each of these starts only when you tap it. A WhatsApp reminder opens WhatsApp with the member’s number and a ready-made message; nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. Statements and summaries can contain members’ names, amounts and turns, and a backup file contains all of your Turnpot data in readable form, so share and store them carefully.

Reminders

Collection-day reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone: one at 9 am on each committee’s collection day, and a follow-up 3 days later if anyone still owes. No push server is involved. You can switch them off in Settings → Collection-day reminders.

No other network use

Apart from the subscription check described above (and Android Auto Backup, which is run by your phone, not by us), Turnpot does not send your data anywhere and has no analytics, crash reporting or advertising software. The links the app opens are WhatsApp (wa.me), Google Play’s subscriptions page, and this website’s privacy policy and terms. Links on this website open in your browser.

3. What we do not collect

  • No account, email address or password. There is no sign-in.
  • No bank logins, card numbers or UPI PINs. The app never collects, holds or moves money.
  • No access to your contacts, camera, location, microphone, SMS, call log, phone state, or photos and other files. Members’ phone numbers are typed in by you. Restoring a backup uses the system file picker, which only gives the app the one file you pick. The only permission Turnpot asks for is notifications, for reminders.
  • No advertising identifiers, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.

4. How long data is kept

Data on your phone stays until you delete it in the app, use Delete all data, or uninstall the app. A copy in your Google account backup is kept and deleted by Google according to your backup settings (see section 1). Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies (for example for tax, accounting and fraud prevention). You can ask us to request deletion of the RevenueCat record linked to your app user ID (see Delete your data).

5. Your choices and rights

You can view, edit, export and delete all of your data in the app at any time. Because we do not hold your data, we cannot view, correct or recover it for you, so please keep a backup. If you are a committee member and want your details changed or removed, please ask your organiser, who keeps them on their own phone. Depending on where you live (for example India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, the UK, the EU or California), you may have additional rights under local law; contact us (see section 8) and we will help where we can.

6. Children

The app is meant for adults who organise a savings circle. It is not intended for children.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and for important changes we will tell you in the app.

8. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues and please say which app: Turnpot. Issues there are public, so please do not include personal details such as members’ names or phone numbers, amounts, statements or your Google Play order number.
