2. Record keeping only

4. Lucky draw

The in-app lucky draw is a random picker that chooses which share receives the pot in a round, from the shares that have not received it yet. It is not a game of chance, betting or a lottery: there are no stakes, no prizes and nothing to win or lose from the draw itself, since every member receives the pot once per share over the life of the committee. Agree with your members beforehand on how the draw will be done.

5. Calculations are aids

Totals, pending amounts, late status, bid payouts and dividends are worked out from the figures you enter, using the rounding rule shown in the app (for example, dividends rounded down to whole rupees, with any leftover added to the winner’s payout). They are aids, not guarantees. Check the figures yourself and with your members before money changes hands. Reminders are notifications on your phone and may not appear if notifications are turned off, the phone is off, or the system delays them.
