Your committee’s hisab, round by round.

Running a committee, kitty or BC with friends, family or colleagues? Turnpot is the organiser’s record book: who has paid this month, who is late, who has already taken the pot and whose turn is next.

Get it on Google Play

Android, coming soon to Google Play. Free for one committee with up to 10 members; Turnpot Pro, an optional subscription, adds unlimited committees, bid rounds, PDF statements and backup files. No ads, no account and no sign-up. English and Hindi.

  • Who paid, who is late

    Mark each member’s contribution with date and amount. Part payments add up, and anyone still owing after the collection day shows as late.

  • A fair lucky draw

    Pick this round’s winner at random from the shares that have not won yet, or follow a fixed order. The result is saved with the time of the draw.

  • Bid rounds worked out

    Enter the winning bid and Turnpot shows the payout and each member’s reduced contribution, so money in always equals money out.

Turnpot only keeps records. It never collects, holds or transfers money, takes no deposits, pays no interest and offers no loans, credit or investments. It is not a chit fund company and does not run, register or guarantee any committee. Members pay the organiser the way they always do; the app is the organiser’s notebook.

Made for the way committees really work

  • Monthly amount per share, number of rounds, start month and collection day.
  • Members with more than one share (for example “2 naam”) get one turn per share.
  • Member ledger: total paid, total received, what is pending, and whether they have won yet.
  • A reminder on your phone on collection day, and again 3 days later if anyone still owes.
  • A polite reminder message for WhatsApp, ready to check and send yourself.
  • A plain-text summary for the group, and round or full statements as PDF.

Your data stays on your phone

There is no account and no Turnpot server. Committees, members, payments and draw results are saved on your phone, and in your own Google account backup if your phone’s backup is on. Use Settings → Backup & restore to save a backup file wherever you like and to move to a new phone.
