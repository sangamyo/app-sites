Share the cost and the work of caring for Mum and Dad, fairly.

One list for every bill, carer invoice and visit. Everyone sees who paid what, who owes whom, and who is taking Dad to the doctor on Tuesday.

Get it on Google Play

Android, coming soon to Google Play. Up to 6 brothers, sisters and spouses per family. Free for up to 15 shared costs a month; one optional Hearthpool Pro subscription covers everyone in the family.

  • Who owes whom, in plain words

    Split each cost equally or by the shares you agreed. Hearthpool shows the fewest payments needed to square up. You pay each other however you like, then tap "record".

  • A visit and task rota

    Doctor's appointments, the weekly shop, Sunday visits. Pick who does each one and, if you turn reminders on, get a reminder on the morning it is your turn.

  • Receipts as proof (Pro)

    Snap a receipt and the amount and date fill in. The photo is read on your phone and stays there. With Pro, export a yearly PDF with totals by person and category.

How it works

  1. One of you starts a care circle with your parent's first name.
  2. Brothers and sisters join with a 6-character invite code.
  3. Add costs and rota tasks as they happen. Settle up whenever you like.

What we don't do

Hearthpool is not a health record and not a bank. We store costs and tasks only. No medical details, no money transfers, no ads.
