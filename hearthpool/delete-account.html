Delete your Hearthpool account

You can delete your Hearthpool account at any time in the app. If you can’t sign in, ask us and we will delete it for you. Deletion cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Right away: your account: email address, name and hashed password. You are removed from your care circle.
  • If you were the only member of your care circle: the circle and all of its costs, payment records, notes and rota tasks are deleted right away too.
  • If other family members are still in the circle: the shared costs and payment records you were part of stay available to them, because they are also their record and their balances depend on them. Your name in that ledger is replaced with "Deleted member". Your open rota tasks become unassigned.
  • Receipt photos: we never stored them. They stay on your phone; deleting your account in the app, signing out or uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Purchase records: Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies (for example for tax and fraud prevention). To ask us to request deletion of the RevenueCat record, open an issue as described under “Can’t sign in?” below (no personal details). Details are in our privacy policy.
Have a subscription? Deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel first in the Google Play Store app (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) or at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions. One subscription covers the whole care circle, so check with your family before cancelling.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Hearthpool on your phone.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Read the message and tap Delete account again to confirm. Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

Deleting your account in the app also removes the receipt photos and reminders kept on that phone.

Can’t sign in?

  1. Open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues titled “Hearthpool account deletion”, so we know which app it is.
  2. Do not post your email address, password or any other personal details there. Issues are public.
  3. We will reply on the issue with a private way to confirm the account is yours, then delete it as described above, usually within 30 days.
