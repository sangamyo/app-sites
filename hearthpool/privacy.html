Privacy policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what information the Hearthpool Android app and this website (together, "the app") collect, why, who else handles it, and how you can delete it. The app is made by an independent developer, the developer named on Hearthpool’s Google Play listing ("we", "us").

The short version. Hearthpool stores your family's shared care costs and tasks, not health records. We never connect to a bank and no money moves through the app. Receipt photos stay on your phone. We do not sell your data and the app shows no ads. You can delete your account at any time, in the app or on our account deletion page.

1. What we collect

Account information

  • Your email address and the name you choose. We use them to sign you in and to show your family who added what.
  • Your password, stored only in hashed form. We cannot see it.

Care circle data your family enters

  • The circle name, the first name of the parent you care for (for display only), the currency and the invite code.
  • Shared costs: amount, date, category (for example medicine, carer, rent or bills), a short note, who paid and how it is shared.
  • Payment records: that one member paid another back. This is only a record; no money moves through the app.
  • Rota tasks: a short title, a date, who it is assigned to and whether it is done.

The app is not a health record. It has no fields for diagnoses, conditions or medicines, and we ask you not to type medical details into notes or task titles. This data is stored on our server, which runs on Render and uses a MongoDB Atlas cloud database, so that everyone in your circle sees the same ledger.

Receipt photos

When you snap a receipt, the photo is processed on your phone by Google ML Kit text recognition to fill in the amount and date. We do not upload or store the photo. The app copies the photo into its private storage on your phone, as your own proof; other family members cannot see it. The app’s Android backup settings leave out these photos and your sign-in token, so they are not copied into your Google account backup. The photos are deleted from your phone when you delete the cost, sign out, leave the circle or delete your account. The app uses the camera only when you tap "Snap receipt", and it never asks for access to your photo library.

Google ML Kit may send Google limited technical information, such as device model, app version and performance metrics. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

Reminders

Rota reminders are optional and off until you turn on Morning reminders in the Rota tab; the app asks for notification permission only then. They are local notifications that your phone schedules for itself. We do not use push notifications, and no notification token is sent to us.

Things you choose to share

When you share your circle’s invite code, or export the yearly PDF (made on your phone), the app opens your phone’s share menu and the content goes wherever you choose to send it, under that service’s own privacy policy.

Subscription information

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your care circle has an active subscription. RevenueCat and Google Play receive a circle identifier (a random ID, not your name or email) and purchase information such as product, price, purchase date, trial and renewal status. One subscription covers everyone in the circle.

Technical data

Our server receives your IP address and basic request information when the app talks to it. We use this to keep the service secure, for example to limit repeated sign-in attempts; the server keeps the address in memory for this only for a short time and does not save it in the database. Our hosting provider keeps request logs for a limited time. The app has no analytics or crash reporting software.

What we do not collect

  • No health records, diagnoses or medicine schedules.
  • No bank logins, account numbers or card numbers.
  • No contacts, location, microphone or photo-library access.
  • No advertising identifiers. The app has no ads, analytics or crash reporting.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To share your care circle's ledger and rota with its members.
  • To provide subscription features and check subscription status.
  • To answer your questions and requests.

We do not use your data for advertising and do not give financial, tax or medical advice.

3. Who can see or handle your data

  • Your care circle. Everyone in your circle (up to six people) can see all of its costs, payment records and tasks, including your name.
  • Service providers that run parts of the service for us: Render (server hosting), MongoDB Atlas (database), RevenueCat (subscription status), Google Play (billing) and Google ML Kit (on-device text recognition). They may only use the data to provide their service to us.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Security

Data travels between the app and our server over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored hashed and database access is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

5. How long we keep data

  • We keep your account and care circle data for as long as your account exists.
  • If you leave a care circle, the costs and payments you were part of stay in that family's ledger with your name, so everyone's balances still add up.
  • If you delete your account, your email, name and hashed password are deleted immediately. In the shared ledger, entries you took part in stay for your family but your name is replaced with "Deleted member", and your open rota tasks become unassigned. If you were the last member, the circle and all of its costs, payments and tasks are deleted immediately.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies. Ask us (see section 11) if you want us to request deletion of the RevenueCat record for your circle.
  • Deleted data may remain for a limited time in backups kept by our database provider until they are overwritten.

6. Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account.
  • On the web: our account deletion page. You don't need the app installed.
  • If you can’t sign in: see the account deletion page for how to ask us without posting personal details.

Deleting your account in the app also deletes the receipt photos kept on that phone, cancels your reminders and signs you out. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your circle's data in the app at any time. You can also contact us (see section 11) to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California or India) you may have additional rights; we will answer valid requests, usually within 30 days.

8. Children

The app is for adults caring for a parent. It is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Where data is processed

Our service providers may store and process data in countries other than the one where you live, including the United States.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above, and tell you in the app about important changes.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues and say which app it is about (Hearthpool). Issues there are public, so please do not include personal details such as your email address, your family’s names or amounts from your ledger.
