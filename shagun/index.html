Your family’s shagun register, on your phone.

Write every lifafa at the gift table in seconds, see what each family gave you over the years, and know what to give back when they invite you. A digital vyavhar / neota / moi notebook that never gets lost.

Get it on Google Play

Android, coming soon to Google Play. English and हिन्दी. Free for 2 functions of your own and unlimited gifts you give; GiftBahi Pro, an optional subscription, adds unlimited functions, PDF registers and backup files. No ads, no account and no sign-up.

  • Fast at the gift table

    Collection mode has a big keypad, quick amounts like ₹101, ₹501 and ₹1,100, and names that fill themselves in from your register.

  • “They are inviting you”

    See what a family gave at your function and what you gave at theirs, with a suggested return gift in the auspicious +1 style.

  • Share and search

    Search every gift by name or village. Share a function’s list on WhatsApp, or as a PDF register with GiftBahi Pro.

GiftBahi only keeps records. It never collects, holds or transfers money. Gifts are given the way they always are; the app just remembers them for you.

Your register stays with you

There is no account and no GiftBahi server. Families, functions and gifts are saved on your phone, and in your own Google account backup if your phone’s backup is on. With GiftBahi Pro you can also save a backup file wherever you like (for example Google Drive) to move to a new phone.
