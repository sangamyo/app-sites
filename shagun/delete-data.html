Delete your GiftBahi data

GiftBahi has no accounts and no server that stores your data. Everything you enter is saved on your phone (and, if your phone’s Google backup is on, in your own Google account backup), so you delete it there.

Option 1: Delete all data in the app

  1. Open GiftBahi.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Under Your data, tap Delete all data.
  4. Tap Delete everything, then Yes, delete to confirm.

This immediately and permanently erases every family, function and gift, and your currency setting, from the phone. (In Hindi the buttons are सेटिंग्स → सारा डेटा हटाएँ → सब हटाएँ → हाँ, हटाएँ.) To delete only one family or function, open it and tap the bin icon at the top.

Option 2: Uninstall the app

Uninstalling GiftBahi removes the app and all of its data from your phone. If your phone’s Google backup is turned on, Android may keep a copy of app data in your Google account backup; you can manage or delete it in your phone’s settings under Google → Backup (the exact menu names vary by phone).

Backup files and shared lists

If you exported a backup file (for example to Google Drive, email or WhatsApp), delete that file there too. Gift lists you shared as text or PDF stay wherever you sent them. We never receive any of these.

Subscription records

Deleting data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records linked to a random app user ID (not your name). To ask us to request deletion of the RevenueCat record, open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues titled “GiftBahi subscription record deletion”. Do not post your Google Play order number, email address or any other personal details there. We will reply on the issue with a private way to send the order number (it starts with GPA.), then ask RevenueCat to delete the record, usually within 30 days.

If someone recorded a gift from you

The family that recorded your gift keeps it on their own phone; we cannot see or delete it. Please ask them directly.

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues and please say which app: GiftBahi. Issues there are public, so please do not include personal details.

What is deleted: all app data on the phone, immediately. What may be kept: a copy in your own Google account backup until you delete it or it is replaced, any files or lists you shared yourself, and Google Play / RevenueCat purchase records, kept under their policies for tax, accounting and fraud prevention. We hold no other data about you.
