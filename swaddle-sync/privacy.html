Privacy policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what information the Swaddle Sync Android app and this website (together, "the app") collect, why, who else handles it, and how you can delete it. The app is made by an independent developer, the developer named on Swaddle Sync’s Google Play listing ("we", "us").

The short version. Your baby’s log is private to the family members you invite. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else. The app shows no ads and has no analytics or crash reporting. You can delete your account at any time, in the app or on our account deletion page.

1. What we collect

Account information

  • Your email address and the name you choose, used to sign you in and to show your family who logged each entry.
  • Your password, stored only in hashed form. We cannot see your actual password.

Family and baby data you enter

  • Family name, invite code, and each member’s role (parent or helper).
  • Each baby’s name (or nickname) and birth date.
  • Log entries: feeds (breast side and minutes, bottle amount, solids), sleep times, nappy changes, medicine name and dose as you type it, temperature readings, notes, the time of each entry and who logged it.

Some of this is health-related information about a child. It is stored on our server, which runs on Render and keeps data in a MongoDB Atlas cloud database, so that everyone in the family sees the same log. Entries you make while offline are first saved on your phone and sent when you are back online.

When you delete an entry, the server keeps only its random entry ID (not its content) for up to 120 days, so that a phone that was offline cannot bring the deleted entry back.

Data kept on your phone

To work offline, the app keeps on your phone: your sign-in token (in Android’s secure storage), a copy of your family, babies and recent entries, entries not yet sent to the server, and your display settings (units, night mode and the baby shown). Signing out removes the sign-in token and the copied data; entries not yet sent stay on the phone and are sent when you sign in again with the same account. Deleting your account in the app removes all of it. Android Auto Backup is on for the app, so if your phone’s Google backup is turned on, Android may copy this app data into your own Google account backup, which Google stores under Google’s privacy policy and we never see. You can manage or delete it in your phone’s settings under Google → Backup (the exact menu names vary by phone).

Subscription information

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your family has an active subscription. RevenueCat and Google Play receive a family identifier (an ID our server gives your family, not your name, email or anything about your baby) and purchase information such as the product, price, purchase date, trial status and renewal status. RevenueCat’s software in the app may also send basic technical information it needs to work, such as the app version and the type of device. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Technical data

Our server receives your IP address and basic request information when the app or this website talks to it. The server uses your IP address only briefly, in memory, to limit repeated sign-in, sign-up and account-deletion attempts. Our hosting provider keeps request logs, which can include your IP address, for a limited time to run and secure the service.

What we do not collect

  • No photos, camera, microphone, contacts, files or location. The app does not ask for any of these permissions.
  • No notifications or reminders, and no push token.
  • No advertising identifiers and no ads. No analytics or crash reporting software.
  • No card or bank details. Google Play handles payment.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep it secure.
  • To sync your family’s baby log between the phones of the people you invited.
  • To show daily totals and to create the PDF report on your phone when you ask for it. You decide whom you share that PDF with.
  • To provide subscription features and answer your questions and requests.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or research, and we do not give medical advice.

3. Who can see or handle your data

  • Your family. Everyone in your family (up to 6 people you invited with the code) can see and edit the whole log, including your name on the entries you made. Parents can remove members.
  • Service providers that run parts of the service for us: Render (server hosting), MongoDB Atlas (database), RevenueCat (subscription status) and Google Play (billing). They may only use the data to provide their service to us.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law.

We do not sell your personal information or your baby’s information, and do not share it for advertising.

4. Security

Data travels between the app and our server over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored hashed. Access to the database is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

5. How long we keep data

  • We keep your account and the family log for as long as your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, your email, name and hashed password are deleted immediately and you are removed from the family. Entries you logged stay in the family’s log (they are the baby’s record), but your name is removed from them.
  • If you were the last member of the family, the family, its babies and every entry are deleted immediately.
  • A parent can delete a baby, which deletes every entry for that baby.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies. Contact us (see section 11) if you want us to request deletion of the RevenueCat record.
  • Our database provider may keep backups for a limited time, so deleted data can remain in a backup until it is overwritten.
  • Request logs kept by our hosting provider are deleted after a limited time.

6. Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • If you can’t sign in: open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues titled “Swaddle Sync account deletion”. Do not post your email address, password or any other personal details there; we will reply on the issue with a private way to confirm the account is yours, then delete it, usually within 30 days. The steps are also on our account deletion page.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view, edit and delete entries in the app at any time. You can contact us (see section 11) to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it. Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU or India) you may have more rights under local law; we will answer valid requests, usually within 30 days.

8. Children

The app is for parents and carers, not for children. Accounts are for adults only. The app stores information about a baby that a parent or carer enters; it does not collect information from children.

9. Where data is processed

Our service providers (Render, MongoDB Atlas, RevenueCat and Google) may store and process data in countries other than the one where you live, including the United States.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and for important changes we will tell you in the app.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues and say which app: Swaddle Sync. Issues there are public, so please do not include personal details such as your email address, your baby’s name or birth date, or any log entries.
