Every feed, nap and nappy, on both parents’ phones, even at 3 a.m.

One shared log for the whole family. Tap once to log, see when the baby last ate and who changed the last nappy, and stop asking “when did she last eat?”.

Get it on Google Play

Android, coming soon to Google Play. Free to use, with an optional Pro subscription (monthly or yearly). One subscription covers the whole family: parents, grandparents and the nanny (up to 6 people). No ads.

  • Shared by both parents

    Your partner, grandparents or nanny join with a 6-character code. Every entry shows who logged it.

  • Made for 3 a.m.

    Night mode turns the screen red on black with huge buttons you can hit one-handed. Logs save on the phone even with no signal and sync later.

  • Ready for the doctor

    Daily totals for feeds, bottle amounts, sleep and nappies, and with Pro, a PDF of the last 7 days to share at the check-up.

How it works

  1. One parent creates the family and adds the baby.
  2. Everyone else joins with the invite code.
  3. Tap to log feeds (left, right, bottle, solids), sleep, nappies, medicine and temperature.

The app is a log, not a medical device. It gives no medical advice. Call your doctor if you are worried.
