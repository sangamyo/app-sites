Delete your Swaddle Sync account

You can delete your Swaddle Sync account at any time in the app. If you can’t sign in, ask us and we will delete it for you. Deletion cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Right away: your account — email address, name and hashed password. You are removed from your family.
  • If you were the only member of your family: the family, its babies and every log entry (feeds, sleep, nappies, medicine, temperatures and notes) are deleted right away too.
  • If other family members remain: the baby’s log stays available to them, because it is also their record. Your name is removed from the entries you logged. A parent can delete the baby (and all its entries) in the app.
  • Purchase records: Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies (for example for tax and fraud prevention). Open an issue (see below) if you want us to request deletion of the RevenueCat record. Details are in our privacy policy.
Have a subscription? Deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel first in the Google Play Store app (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) or at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions. If others in your family keep using the app, they may want to keep the subscription.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Swaddle Sync on your phone.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Read the message and tap Delete account again to confirm. Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

Can’t sign in?

  1. Open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues titled “Swaddle Sync account deletion”, so we know which app it is.
  2. Do not post your email address, password or any other personal details there. Issues are public.
  3. We will reply on the issue with a private way to confirm the account is yours, then delete it as described above, usually within 30 days.
