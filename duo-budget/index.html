Budget together with your partner — no bank login, ever.

One shared budget for the two of you. Add an expense in three taps, snap a receipt with Pro, and always know who owes whom.

Get it on Google Play

Android, coming soon to Google Play. Free plan included; Duo Budget Pro is an optional subscription, and one subscription covers both partners. No ads.

  • No bank login

    You never connect a bank or share a bank password. You add what you spend; nothing breaks when a bank changes its login.

  • See who owes whom

    Split each expense equally, keep it personal, or mark it as paid for your partner. One running balance, and a "settle up" button to record payback.

  • Snap a receipt (Pro)

    With Duo Budget Pro, take a photo and the amount, date and store fill in for you. The photo is read on your phone and never uploaded.

How it works

  1. One of you creates a shared household.
  2. The other joins with a 6-character invite code.
  3. Set monthly budgets per category and start adding expenses together.
