Delete your Duo Budget account

You can delete your Duo Budget account at any time in the app. If you can’t sign in, ask us and we will delete it for you. Deletion cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Right away: your account — email address, name and hashed password. You are removed from your household.
  • If you were the only member of your household: the household and all of its expenses, budgets, settlements, notes and merchant names are deleted right away too.
  • If your partner is still in the household: the shared household data stays available to them, because it is also their record. They can delete it by deleting their own account or removing items in the app.
  • Receipt photos: we never stored them. They are read on your phone only.
  • Purchase records: Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies (for example for tax and fraud prevention). If you want us to request deletion of the RevenueCat record, open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues (no personal details needed). Details are in our privacy policy.
Have a subscription? Deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel first in the Google Play Store app (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) or at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions. If your partner keeps using the household, they may want to keep the subscription.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Duo Budget on your phone.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Read the message and tap Delete account again to confirm. Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

Can’t sign in?

  1. Open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues titled “Duo Budget account deletion”, so we know which app it is.
  2. Do not post your email address, password or any other personal details there. Issues are public.
  3. We will reply on the issue with a private way to confirm the account is yours, then delete it as described above, usually within 30 days.
