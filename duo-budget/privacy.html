Privacy policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what information the Duo Budget Android app and this website (together, "the app") collect, why, who else handles it, and how you can delete it. The app is made by an independent developer, the developer named on Duo Budget’s Google Play listing ("we", "us").

The short version. We never ask for your bank login and never connect to your bank. We do not sell your data, and the app shows no ads. Receipt photos are read on your phone and are not uploaded. You can delete your account at any time, in the app or on our account deletion page.

1. What we collect

Account information

  • Your email address and the name you choose, used to sign you in and to show your partner who added what.
  • Your password, which we store only in hashed form. We cannot see your actual password.

Household data you enter

  • Household name, currency and invite code.
  • Expenses: amount, category, date, who paid, how it is split, merchant (store) name and any note you add.
  • Monthly budgets per category.
  • Settlements (records that one partner paid the other back). No money moves through the app.

This data is stored on our server, which runs on Render and uses a cloud database (MongoDB Atlas), so that both partners see the same budget on their phones. On your phone, the app keeps only your sign-in token, in the phone’s secure storage, until you sign out.

Receipt photos

When you scan a receipt, the photo is processed on your device by Google ML Kit text recognition to fill in the amount, date and merchant. We do not upload or store the photo. Only the details you confirm and save become part of the expense. Receipt scanning is part of Duo Budget Pro. The app uses the camera only when you choose to scan a receipt; if you pick an existing photo instead, Android’s photo picker gives the app only the one photo you choose.

Google ML Kit may send Google limited technical information, such as device model, app version and performance metrics, to help Google run and improve the feature. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

Subscription information

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your household has an active subscription. RevenueCat and Google Play receive a household identifier (a random ID, not your name or email) and purchase information such as the product, price, purchase date, trial status and renewal status. One subscription covers both members of a household. Our server stores your household’s plan (Free or Pro) and when it expires, which it receives from RevenueCat.

Crash reports and usage analytics

The app includes Google’s Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase software, but in the current version it is turned off. If we turn it on in a later version, the app will send crash reports and basic usage events (for example that an expense was added or a receipt was scanned, plus device model, OS version and an app-instance identifier) to Google Firebase. These events never include amounts, names, email addresses, notes or merchant names, and advertising ID collection is turned off. We would use this only to fix bugs and improve the app, never for advertising, and we will update this policy before it is turned on.

Technical data

Like any online service, our server receives your IP address and basic request information when the app or this website talks to it. Our server does not keep a log of your requests; it holds IP addresses briefly in memory to limit repeated sign-in and account deletion attempts. Our hosting provider keeps basic request logs (such as IP address and time) for a limited time to run and secure the service.

What we do not collect

  • No bank logins, bank account numbers or card numbers.
  • No access to your contacts, location or microphone, and no access to your whole photo library. The only permission the app asks for is the camera, for receipt scanning.
  • No advertising identifiers. The app has no ads.
  • No receipt photos. They are read on your phone and never uploaded.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To sync your household budget between you and your partner.
  • To provide subscription features and check your subscription status.
  • To answer questions and requests you send us.
  • If crash reports or analytics are turned on in a later version: to find and fix problems.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not give financial advice.

3. Who can see or handle your data

  • Your partner. Everyone in your household (at most two people) can see all household data, including expenses you add and your name.
  • Service providers that run parts of the service for us: Render (server hosting), MongoDB Atlas (database), RevenueCat (subscription status), Google Play (billing), Google ML Kit (on-device text recognition) and, only if turned on in a later version, Google Firebase (crash reports and analytics). They may only use the data to provide their service to us.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of users.

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with anyone for advertising.

4. Security

Data travels between the app and our server over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored hashed. Access to the database is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

5. How long we keep data

  • We keep your account and household data for as long as your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, your account (email, name, hashed password) is deleted immediately and you are removed from your household.
  • If your partner is still in the household, the shared household data (expenses, budgets, settlements and notes) stays available to them, because it is also their data. If you were the last member, the household and all of its expenses, budgets and settlements are deleted immediately.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies, for example for tax, accounting and fraud prevention. You can ask us (see Contact) to request deletion of the RevenueCat record for your household.
  • If our database provider keeps backup copies, deleted data can remain in them for a limited time until those backups expire.

6. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: open the Settings tab and, under Account, tap Delete account.
  • On the web: use our account deletion page. You don't need the app installed.
  • If you cannot sign in: open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues titled “Duo Budget account deletion”. Do not post your email address or any other personal details there; we will reply on the issue with a private way to confirm the account is yours.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. To stop being charged, cancel it in the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your household data in the app at any time. You can also contact us (see section 11) to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it; we will reply with a private way to confirm the account is yours. Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU or India), you may have additional rights under local law; we will honor valid requests, usually within 30 days.

8. Children

The app is meant for adults managing a household budget. It is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us (see section 11) and we will delete it.

9. Where data is processed

Our service providers (Render, MongoDB Atlas, RevenueCat and Google) may store and process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. We rely on these providers' safeguards when data moves between countries.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and for important changes we will tell you in the app or by email.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues and say which app it is about (Duo Budget). Issues there are public, so please do not include personal details such as your email address, your partner’s name or your expenses.
