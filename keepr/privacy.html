Privacy policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what information the Keepr Android app and this website (together, "the app") handle. The app is made by an independent developer, the developer named on Keepr’s Google Play listing ("we", "us").

The short version. Keepr has no accounts and no server of its own. Everything you enter, including items, receipts, photos and reminders, is stored on your phone. Receipt photos are read on your phone by Google ML Kit. We cannot see any of it. If your phone’s Google backup is turned on, Android may copy your item list (not your photos) into your own Google account backup. Apart from that, the only information that leaves your phone is subscription purchase data handled by Google Play and RevenueCat, and limited technical information that Google ML Kit may send to Google. The app shows no ads, has no analytics and we do not sell data.

1. Data stored on your phone

  • Items: item name, store, purchase date, price, category, notes and serial number.
  • Return and warranty details: return window, warranty length and any extended warranty end date.
  • Photos you add: receipts, warranty cards, product labels or serial plates, and manuals.
  • Your settings: whether reminders are on, your currency, and the store return-window presets you edit.

This data is kept in a database and in the app’s private files on your phone. It is not uploaded to us or anyone else. If you uninstall the app or use Delete all data, it is erased from the phone (see Delete your data, and Android device backup below).

Android device backup (your Google account)

Android device backup may include your item list but not photos, so use Keepr’s own Backup & restore for photos. If backup is turned on in your phone’s settings, Android may copy Keepr’s database (your items, their details and your settings) to your Google account backup, or to a new phone during setup, so your list comes back on a new phone. Photos are always left out of it. That backup is made and stored by Android and Google under your Google account and your phone’s settings (see Google’s privacy policy); we never receive it. A copy there may remain after you delete data in the app or uninstall it, until Android updates or removes that backup. You can turn device backup off or delete it in your phone’s settings (usually Settings → Google → Backup; the exact menu names vary by phone).

2. Data that leaves your phone

Subscriptions (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription. RevenueCat and Google Play receive a random app user ID created on your phone (not your name, phone or email) and purchase information such as the product, price, purchase date, trial status and renewal status, plus basic device information (for example the app version, OS version and country/store). See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy.

Things you choose to share

When you share a photo, copy or share the claim text, or export a backup file, the content goes wherever you choose to send it (for example email, WhatsApp or Google Drive), under that service’s own privacy policy. A backup file contains all of your Keepr data, including your photos, in readable form. Store it somewhere safe.

No other network use

Keepr does not send your items, receipts or photos anywhere and has no analytics, crash reporting or advertising software. Links in the app (this privacy policy, the terms and Google Play’s subscription page) open in your browser.

3. Camera, photos and receipt reading

  • The app uses the camera only when you tap Take photo to photograph a receipt or document. It asks for camera permission the first time you do this.
  • If you choose a picture from your phone, Keepr uses the system photo picker and receives only the photo you pick. It does not ask for access to your photo library or other files and does not browse them. Restoring a backup uses the system file picker, which only gives the app the one file you pick.
  • Receipt text is read on your phone by Google ML Kit text recognition to suggest the store, purchase date and total. The photo and its text are not sent to us or to any AI service. You check the details before they are saved.
  • Google ML Kit may send Google limited technical information such as device model, app version and performance metrics; see Google’s ML Kit data disclosure.

4. Reminders

Return and warranty reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone: 3 days before a return window closes, and 30 and 7 days before a warranty ends. No push server is involved. Reminders are on by default; the app asks for notification permission after you save your first item, and you can switch reminders off in Settings at any time.

5. What we do not collect

  • No account, email address or password. There is no sign-in.
  • No bank links, card numbers or email inbox access. Keepr does not import receipts from email.
  • No cloud sync. Nothing is stored on a Keepr server, because there isn’t one.
  • No access to your contacts, location, microphone, or photo library and other files. The only permissions the app asks for are the camera (when you tap Take photo) and notifications for its reminders.
  • No advertising identifiers, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.

6. How long data is kept

Data on your phone stays until you delete it in the app, use Delete all data, or uninstall the app. A copy in your Google account backup is kept and deleted by Google according to your backup settings (see section 1). Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies (for example for tax, accounting and fraud prevention). You can ask us to request deletion of the RevenueCat record linked to your app user ID (see Delete your data).

7. Your choices and rights

You can view, edit, export and delete all of your data in the app at any time. Because we do not hold your data, we cannot view, correct or recover it for you, so please keep a backup. Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU or India), you may have additional rights under local law; contact us (see section 10) and we will help where we can.

8. Children

The app is meant for adults keeping track of their purchases. It is not intended for children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and for important changes we will tell you in the app.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues and please say which app: Keepr. Issues there are public, so please do not include personal details such as serial numbers, receipts, photos or your Google Play order number.
