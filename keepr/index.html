Snap the receipt once. Never miss a return or warranty again.

For the TV, the fridge, the sofa and everything else you might need to return or repair. Keepr keeps the receipt and reminds you before time runs out.

Get it on Google Play

Android, coming soon to Google Play. Free for up to 10 items; Pro unlocks unlimited items and extra photos. No ads, no account and no sign-up.

  • Snap and fill in

    Take a photo of the receipt. Keepr reads it on your phone and fills in the store, date and total for you to check.

  • Reminders before it’s too late

    A reminder 3 days before the return window closes, and 30 days and 7 days before the warranty ends.

  • Claim in one tap

    Item, serial number, purchase date, store and price, ready to copy or share when you contact the store or maker.

How it works

  1. Snap the receipt, or pick the photo from your phone.
  2. Check the details and set the return window and warranty length.
  3. Keepr shows what has to go back soon and which warranties are ending.

Your data stays on your phone

There is no account and no Keepr server. Items, receipts and photos are saved on your phone, and receipts are read on your phone too. If your phone’s Google backup is on, Android may also keep your item list (not photos) in your own Google account backup. Use Backup & restore in Settings to save a backup file with your photos wherever you like (for example Google Drive) and to move to a new phone.
