Plan the wedding budget together, with your partner and both families, on one screen.

Every vendor, every deposit and balance, who paid what, and how many guests are coming to each event.

Get it on Google Play

Android, coming soon to Google Play. Free for up to 3 vendors; one in-app purchase unlocks the whole wedding. No subscription, no ads.

  • Planned vs paid vs still owed

    Add the venue, caterer, photographer and the rest with what you planned to spend. See what is paid and what is still owed, overall and by category.

  • Never miss a payment

    Put in deposit and balance due dates. Your phone reminds you 7 days and 1 day before each one.

  • Both families, one budget

    The couple plus up to 4 helpers join with a code. Mark each payment as the bride's side, the groom's side or the couple, and see the running totals.

  • Guests for every event

    Mehendi, Sangeet, Ceremony, Reception: track RSVPs and party sizes, and see the head count for each event.

  • Snap a receipt or quote

    Take a photo and the amount fills in. The photo is read on your phone and never uploaded.

How it works

  1. One of you creates the wedding and picks the events.
  2. Your partner and family join with a 6-character invite code.
  3. Add vendors, payments and guests together.
