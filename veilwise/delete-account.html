Delete your Veilwise account

You can delete your Veilwise account at any time in the app. If you can’t sign in, ask us and we will delete it for you. Deletion cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Right away: your account (email address, name and hashed password). You are removed from your wedding.
  • If you were the only member of the wedding: the wedding and all of its events, vendors, payments, guests and notes are deleted right away too.
  • If other members are still in the wedding: the shared wedding data stays available to them, because it is also their record. They can delete it by deleting their own accounts or removing items in the app.
  • Receipt and quote photos: we never stored them. They are read on your phone only.
  • Payment reminders live on your phone. Signing out cancels them; you can also uninstall the app.
  • Purchase records: Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies (for example for tax and fraud prevention). Contact us (see below) if you want us to request deletion of the RevenueCat record. Details are in our privacy policy.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Veilwise on your phone.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Read the message and tap Delete account again to confirm. Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

Can’t sign in?

  1. Open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues titled “Veilwise account deletion”, so we know which app it is.
  2. Do not post your email address, password or any other personal details there. Issues are public.
  3. We will reply on the issue with a private way to confirm the account is yours, then delete it as described above, usually within 30 days.
