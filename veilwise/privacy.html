Privacy policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what information the Veilwise Android app and this website (together, "the app") collect, why, who else handles it, and how you can delete it. The app is made by an independent developer, the developer named on Veilwise’s Google Play listing ("we", "us").

The short version. The app records your wedding budget and guest list; it never moves money. Your account and the shared wedding data are stored on our server so that everyone in the wedding sees the same data. We do not sell your data and the app shows no ads. Receipt and quote photos are read on your phone and are not uploaded. Payment reminders are created on your phone. You can delete your account at any time, in the app or on our account deletion page.

1. What we collect

Account information

  • Your email address and the name you choose, used to sign you in and to show the other members who is in the wedding.
  • Your password, stored only in hashed form. We cannot see your actual password.

Wedding data you enter

  • Wedding name, date, currency and invite code.
  • Events (for example Mehendi or Reception) and their dates.
  • Vendors: name, category, planned amount, linked event and notes.
  • Payments: amount, due date, paid date, which side paid (bride's side, groom's side, couple or other) and any note. No money moves through the app.
  • Guests: the names you enter, their side, RSVP, party size, the events they are invited to and any note. Guests are not contacted by the app.

This data is stored on our server, which uses a cloud database (MongoDB Atlas), so that everyone in the wedding sees the same data on their phones.

Guest names are personal information about other people. Please only add what you need to plan the wedding.

On your phone

The app keeps your sign-in token and your reminder setting in the phone’s secure storage. Wedding data is loaded from our server while you use the app and is not saved in a separate database on the phone. Signing out removes the sign-in token and cancels the payment reminders on that phone.

Receipt and quote photos

When you snap a receipt or quote, the photo is processed on your device by Google ML Kit text recognition to fill in the amount. We do not upload or store the photo. Only the details you confirm and save become part of the payment, together with a note that the amount came from a receipt. The app uses the camera only when you tap the snap button, and it never reads your photo library.

Google ML Kit may send Google limited technical information, such as device model, app version and performance metrics. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

Payment reminders

Reminders at 9:00 in the morning, 7 days and 1 day before each unpaid due date, are scheduled as local notifications on your phone (on the phone of each member who has reminders on). We do not run a push notification service and do not collect a push token. The app asks for notification permission the first time it has a reminder to schedule, and you can turn reminders off in Settings → Reminders.

Purchase information

The one-time unlock is sold and billed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your wedding is unlocked; the app talks to RevenueCat directly, and our own server does not receive purchase information. RevenueCat and Google Play receive a wedding identifier (a random ID, not your name or email) and purchase information such as the product, price and purchase date. One purchase unlocks the wedding for all its members.

Technical data

Our server receives your IP address and basic request information when the app or this website talks to it. The server uses the IP address only in memory, to limit repeated sign-in and account deletion attempts, and does not save it in our database. Our hosting provider keeps request logs for a limited time to run and secure the service.

What we do not collect

  • No bank logins, bank account numbers or card numbers.
  • No access to your contacts, location, microphone or photo library.
  • No advertising identifiers. The app has no ads.
  • No analytics or crash reporting software.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep it secure.
  • To sync the wedding between its members.
  • To check whether your wedding is unlocked.
  • To answer questions and requests you send us.

We do not use your data for advertising and do not give financial advice.

3. Who can see or handle your data

  • Members of your wedding. Everyone who joined with the invite code (at most 6 people) can see and edit all wedding data, and sees your name.
  • Service providers that run parts of the service for us: Render (server hosting), MongoDB Atlas (database), RevenueCat (unlock status), Google Play (billing) and Google ML Kit (on-device text recognition). They may only use the data to provide their service to us.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of users.

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for advertising.

4. Security

Data travels between the app and our server over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored hashed. Access to the database is restricted. Please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

5. How long we keep data

  • We keep your account and wedding data for as long as your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, your account (email, name, hashed password) is deleted immediately and you are removed from the wedding.
  • If other members are still in the wedding, the shared data (events, vendors, payments, guests) stays available to them, because it is also their record. If you were the last member, the wedding and all of its data are deleted immediately.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies (for example for tax and fraud prevention). You can contact us (see section 11) to ask us to request deletion of the RevenueCat record.
  • Deleted data may remain for a limited time in backups kept by our database provider, until those backups are replaced.

6. Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • If you can’t sign in: open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues titled “Veilwise account deletion”. Do not post your email address, password or any other personal details there; we will reply on the issue with a private way to confirm the account is yours, then delete it, usually within 30 days. The steps are also on our account deletion page.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your wedding data in the app at any time. You can also contact us (see section 11) to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it. Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU, the UK or India), you may have more rights under local law; we will honor valid requests and reply within 30 days.

8. Children

The app is meant for adults planning a wedding. It is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Where data is processed

Our service providers may store and process data in countries other than the one where you live, including the United States.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and for important changes we will tell you in the app.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues and say which app: Veilwise. Issues there are public, so please do not include personal details such as your email address, guest names or payment amounts. If we need to confirm that an account is yours, we will reply on the issue with a private way to do that.
