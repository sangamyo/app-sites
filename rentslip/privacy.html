Privacy policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what information the RentSlip Android app and this website (together, "the app") handle. The app is made by an independent developer, the developer named on RentSlip’s Google Play listing ("we", "us").

The short version. RentSlip has no accounts and no server of its own. Everything you enter, including properties, tenants, payments, receipts, meter readings and your landlord profile, is stored on your phone. We cannot see it. If your phone’s Google backup is turned on, Android may copy the app’s data into your own Google account backup, which Google stores and you control in your phone’s settings. Subscription purchase data is handled by Google Play and RevenueCat. The app shows no ads and we do not sell data.

1. Data stored on your phone

  • Your landlord profile: name, address, phone number and, if you add it, your PAN. These are printed on the rent receipts you create.
  • Properties and units: names, addresses and electricity rates.
  • Tenants: name, phone number, tenancy dates, rent (and the months from which it changed), due day and deposit held.
  • Payments you record (amount, date, mode such as cash or UPI, and an optional note), charges such as electricity, rent receipts you issue and meter readings.
  • Your preferences: currency and whether rent-day reminders are on.

This data is kept in a database inside the app’s private storage on your phone. It is never uploaded to us. If you uninstall the app or use Delete all data, it is erased (see Delete your data).

Android Auto Backup (your Google account)

RentSlip keeps Android Auto Backup enabled so your records can survive a lost or replaced phone. If backup is turned on in your phone’s settings, Android may copy the app’s data (including the database above) into your own Google account backup, stored by Google under Google’s privacy policy. We never receive or see this backup. When you set up a new phone with the same Google account and reinstall RentSlip, Android can restore it. To turn this off, go to your phone’s Settings → Google → Backup (the exact menu names vary by phone) and switch off backup; you can also delete existing backups there.

You are responsible for tenants’ information you enter. Only record what you need to manage the tenancy, and tell your tenants that you keep these records.

2. Data that leaves your phone

Subscriptions (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription. RevenueCat and Google Play receive a random app user ID created on your phone (not your name, phone or email) and purchase information such as the product, price, purchase date, trial status and renewal status, plus basic device information (for example the app version, OS version and country/store). See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy.

Things you choose to share

When you share a rent receipt PDF, send a reminder through WhatsApp or another app, or export a backup file, the content goes wherever you choose to send it (for example WhatsApp, email or Google Drive), under that service’s own privacy policy. A backup file contains all of your RentSlip data in readable form. Store it somewhere safe.

Meter photos (on-device text recognition)

When you photograph an electricity meter, the photo is read on your phone by Google ML Kit text recognition to suggest the reading. The photo is not uploaded and is not saved with your RentSlip records. The text-recognition model is downloaded to your phone by Google Play services. Google ML Kit may send Google limited technical information such as device model, app version and performance metrics; see Google’s ML Kit data disclosure. The app uses the camera only when you tap to photograph a meter.

Reminders

Rent-day reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone: one on each tenant’s due day, and another 3 days later if the rent is not fully paid. No push server is involved. The app asks for notification permission after you add your first tenant or when you turn reminders on, and you can switch them off in Settings → Rent-day reminders.

No other network use

Apart from the subscription check and ML Kit described above, RentSlip does not send your data anywhere and has no analytics, crash reporting or advertising software. The links the app opens are WhatsApp (when you tap Remind tenant; nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp), Google Play’s subscriptions page, and this website’s privacy policy and terms. Links on this website open in your browser.

3. What we do not collect

  • No account, email address or password. There is no sign-in.
  • No bank logins, card numbers or UPI PINs. The app never collects or moves money.
  • No access to your contacts, location, microphone, SMS, phone state, or photos and other files. Tenants’ phone numbers are typed in by you. Restoring a backup uses the system file picker, which only gives the app the one file you pick. The camera is used only when you tap to photograph a meter, and notifications only for reminders.
  • No advertising identifiers, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.

4. How long data is kept

Data on your phone stays until you delete it in the app, use Delete all data, or uninstall the app. A copy in your Google account backup is kept and deleted by Google according to your backup settings (see section 1). Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies (for example for tax, accounting and fraud prevention). You can ask us to request deletion of the RevenueCat record linked to your app user ID (see Delete your data).

5. Your choices and rights

You can view, edit, export and delete all of your data in the app at any time. Because we do not hold your data, we cannot view, correct or recover it for you, so please keep a backup. Depending on where you live (for example India, the EU or California), you may have additional rights under local law; contact us (see section 8) and we will help where we can.

6. Children

The app is meant for adults who rent out property. It is not intended for children.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and for important changes we will tell you in the app.

8. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues and please say which app: RentSlip. Issues there are public, so please do not include personal details such as tenants’ names or phone numbers, your PAN, receipts or your Google Play order number.
