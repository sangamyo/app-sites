Know who paid rent, who is late, and send a proper receipt in one tap.

For landlords with a few flats, rooms or PG beds. No more notebooks: one screen shows this month’s rent for every tenant.

Get it on Google Play

Android, coming soon to Google Play. Free for one unit; RentSlip Pro, an optional subscription, unlocks unlimited units. No ads, no account and no sign-up.

  • Rent board

    Paid, partly paid, due or late for every tenant, every month, with totals collected against expected.

  • Rent receipts as PDF

    Monthly or for a whole financial year, with receipt number, your name and PAN, amount in words. Share on WhatsApp or email.

  • Reminders and meters

    A reminder on each due day, a ready-made WhatsApp message for the tenant, and electricity bills from a meter photo.

RentSlip only keeps records. It never collects, holds or transfers rent or any other money, and it does not offer loans or credit. Tenants pay you the way they always do.

Your data stays on your phone

There is no account and no RentSlip server. Properties, tenants, payments and receipts are saved on your phone, and in your own Google account backup if your phone’s backup is on. Use Settings → Backup & restore to save a backup file wherever you like (for example Google Drive) and to move to a new phone.
