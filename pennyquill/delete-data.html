Delete your Pennyquill data

Pennyquill has no accounts and no server that stores your data. Everything you enter is saved on your phone (and, if your phone’s Google backup is on, in your own Google account backup), so you delete it there yourself at any time — we never had a copy.

Option 1: Delete all data in the app

  1. Open Pennyquill.
  2. Tap the Settings icon (the gear at the top right of the Home, Invoices, Clients, Expenses or Tax screen).
  3. Under Your data, tap Delete all data.
  4. Tap Continue, then Delete everything to confirm.

This immediately and permanently erases your business profile, logo, clients, invoices, payments, expenses, receipt photos, settings and scheduled reminders from the phone. The app returns to its first-run screen. Only a count of how many invoices have been created on this phone is kept, so the free plan’s 3 free invoices are not reset; it contains no invoice details. Uninstalling the app (option 2) removes that too.

Option 2: Uninstall the app

Uninstalling Pennyquill from your phone deletes all of its data as well. You can also use Android Settings › Apps › Pennyquill › Storage › Clear storage.

Your Google account backup

If your phone’s Google backup is turned on, Android may keep a copy of Pennyquill’s data in your own Google account backup. You can manage or delete it in your phone’s settings under Settings → Google → Backup (the exact menu names vary by phone). We cannot see or delete that copy for you.

Backup files, invoices and messages

If you made backup files (Settings › Backup & restore), they are wherever you saved them — for example Google Drive, email or your Downloads folder. Delete them there. Invoice PDFs and reminder messages you sent stay wherever you sent them. We never receive any of these.

Subscription records

Deleting data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel Pennyquill Pro in the Google Play Store app › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records (linked to an anonymous ID, not your name) under their own policies, for example for tax and fraud prevention. To ask us to request deletion of your RevenueCat record, open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues titled “Pennyquill purchase record deletion”. Do not post your order ID, email address or any other personal details there. We will reply on the issue with a private way to send us the order ID from your Google Play receipt, then ask RevenueCat to delete the record, usually within 30 days.

Other questions: open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues and please say which app: Pennyquill. Issues there are public, so please do not include personal details.

What is deleted: all app data on the phone, immediately (except the free-invoice count described above). What may be kept: a copy in your own Google account backup until you delete it or it is replaced, backup files and invoices you shared yourself, and purchase records held by Google Play and RevenueCat. We hold no data about you.
