Privacy policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what information the Pennyquill Android app and this website (together, "the app") handle and where it goes. The app is made by an independent developer, the developer named on Pennyquill’s Google Play listing ("we", "us").

The short version. Pennyquill has no account and no server of its own. Everything you enter is stored on your phone, and we cannot see your clients, invoices, expenses or receipt photos. If your phone’s Google backup is turned on, Android may copy the app’s data into your own Google account backup (see section 1); you can turn this off in your phone’s Settings → Google → Backup. Apart from that, the only data that leaves your phone through the app is purchase information handled by Google Play and RevenueCat, limited technical information that Google ML Kit may send to Google (section 2), and anything you choose to share yourself (for example an invoice PDF or a backup file). The app shows no ads, has no analytics, and we do not sell data.

1. Data stored on your phone

The app stores the following in its private storage on your device. It is not sent to us and we have no copy of it:

  • Your business profile: name, address, email, phone, logo, payment instructions (such as bank or UPI details shown on invoices), invoice numbering and, in India mode, your GSTIN and state.
  • Your clients: name, email, phone, address and, in India mode, GSTIN and state.
  • Invoices, line items, payments you record, and invoice status.
  • Expenses and receipt photos you take or choose.
  • Settings such as your tax set-aside percentage and reminder choice, and a count of how many invoices have been created on this phone (used for the free plan).

The app never asks for a bank login, never connects to a bank and does not process payments.

Client names, emails, phone numbers and addresses are about other people. Only record what you need for your invoices.

Android Auto Backup (your Google account)

Pennyquill keeps Android Auto Backup enabled so your invoices and records can survive a lost or replaced phone. If backup is turned on in your phone’s settings, Android may copy the app’s data (including the information above) into your own Google account backup, stored by Google under Google’s privacy policy. We never receive or see this backup. When you set up a new phone with the same Google account and reinstall Pennyquill, Android can restore it. To turn this off, go to your phone’s Settings → Google → Backup (the exact menu names vary by phone) and switch off backup; you can also delete existing backups there. We still recommend saving a backup file (Settings › Backup & restore), because Google’s backup is not guaranteed to include everything or to be on.

2. Receipt photos and text recognition

When you snap a receipt, the photo is read on your device by Google ML Kit text recognition to fill in the amount, date and merchant. The photo is kept in the app’s storage on your phone and is not uploaded by us. The app uses the camera only when you tap Take photo on an expense, and asks for camera permission only then. Choosing a logo or a receipt from your gallery (Choose photo) uses the Android system photo picker, which gives the app only the photo you pick.

The text-recognition model is downloaded to your phone by Google Play services. Google ML Kit may send Google limited technical information, such as device model, app version and performance metrics. See Google’s ML Kit data disclosure.

3. Subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active Pennyquill Pro subscription. RevenueCat and Google Play receive a random, anonymous app-user identifier created by RevenueCat (not your name or email), device and app information needed for the purchase, and purchase information such as the product, price, purchase date, trial status and renewal status. See the RevenueCat privacy policy and the Google privacy policy.

4. Things you share yourself

When you send an invoice PDF, a payment reminder or a backup file, the app opens the Android share sheet and you choose where it goes (for example email, WhatsApp or Google Drive). That app or service then handles the content under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent until you choose an app and send it there. We never receive a copy.

5. Reminders

Tax due-date reminders are optional and off until you turn them on in the Tax tab. They are local notifications scheduled on your phone, 7 days before each estimated-tax (US) or advance-tax (India) date. No push service or server is involved. The app asks for notification permission only when you turn reminders on, and can re-schedule them after your phone restarts.

6. No other network use

Apart from RevenueCat and Google Play (section 3) and Google ML Kit (section 2), the app does not send data anywhere and has no analytics, crash reporting or advertising software. The only links the app opens are this privacy policy, the terms of use and Google Play’s subscriptions page, and they open in your browser or the Play Store app.

7. What we do not collect

  • No account, email address or password. There is no sign-in.
  • No bank logins or card numbers. The app never collects or moves money.
  • No access to your contacts, location, microphone, or photos and files beyond the one photo or backup file you pick. Client details are typed in by you. The only permissions the app asks for are the camera (when you take a receipt photo) and notifications (when you turn tax reminders on).
  • No advertising identifiers and no ads.
  • No analytics or crash reporting.

8. Deleting your data

Your data is stored on your phone, so you control it. In the app, open Settings › Delete all data to erase everything, or uninstall the app. Backup files you saved elsewhere (for example in Google Drive) must be deleted there. A copy in your Google account backup is kept and deleted by Google according to your backup settings (see section 1); manage it in your phone’s Settings → Google → Backup. See Delete your data. Deleting data does not cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel it in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions. To ask us to request deletion of your RevenueCat purchase record, see Subscription records.

9. Security

Your data is protected by your phone’s own security (screen lock and Android app sandboxing). Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app, so use a screen lock. Backup files are plain JSON and are not encrypted: keep them somewhere private.

10. Your choices and rights

You can view, edit, export and delete all of your data in the app at any time. Because we do not hold your data, we cannot view, correct or recover it for you, so please keep a backup. Depending on where you live (for example India, the EU or California), you may have additional rights under local law; contact us (see section 13) and we will help where we can.

11. Children

The app is meant for adults running a business. It is not intended for children.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

13. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues and please say which app: Pennyquill. Issues there are public, so please do not include personal details such as your email address, client names, invoice amounts, your GSTIN or your Google Play order ID.
