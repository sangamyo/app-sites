Apps by the developer

Small Android apps for households, families and small businesses, made by one independent developer: the developer named on each app’s Google Play listing. Each app below has its own home page, privacy policy and terms of use.

  • Duo Budget

    A shared budget for couples: joint expenses, monthly budgets and who owes whom, without a bank login.

    Home Privacy Terms Google Play

  • Veilwise

    Wedding budget and guest planner for the couple and both families: vendors, payment due dates and guest counts.

    Home Privacy Terms Google Play

  • Swaddle Sync

    One baby log both parents share: feeds, naps and nappies on both phones.

    Home Privacy Terms Google Play

  • Hearthpool

    A shared ledger for brothers and sisters caring for a parent: care costs, who owes whom and a visit rota.

    Home Privacy Terms Google Play

  • RentSlip

    Rent tracker and rent receipts for small landlords. Data stays on your phone.

    Home Privacy Terms Google Play

  • Pennyquill

    Invoices and tax set-aside for freelancers. Data stays on your phone.

    Home Privacy Terms Google Play

  • Keepr

    Receipt and warranty vault with reminders before return windows and warranties end. Data stays on your phone.

    Home Privacy Terms Google Play

  • FeeDesk

    Fees and attendance for small gyms, studios and tuition classes. Data stays on your phone.

    Home Privacy Terms Google Play

  • CareNotes

    Doctor-visit notes, medications and tasks for families caring for a parent.
