Delete your Tolanama data

Tolanama has no accounts and no server that stores your data. Everything you enter is saved on your phone (and, if your phone’s Google backup is on, the list without photos is in your own Google account backup), so you delete it there.

Option 1: Delete all data in the app

  1. Open Tolanama.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Under Your data, tap Delete all data.
  4. Tap Delete, then Yes, delete everything to confirm.

This immediately and permanently erases every jewellery item, photo, bill photo, family member, rate and setting from the phone, including the app’s temporary copies of shared files and camera photos.

Option 2: Uninstall the app

Uninstalling Tolanama removes the app and all of its data from your phone.

Your Google account backup

If your phone’s Google backup is turned on, Android may keep a copy of the app’s list (items, weights, prices, family members, places and rates — not photos) in your Google account backup. You can manage or delete it in your phone’s settings under Google → Backup (the exact menu names vary by phone), or at drive.google.com/drive/backups.

Backup files, PDFs and photos you shared

If you exported a backup file or shared an inventory PDF or a photo (for example to Google Drive, email or WhatsApp), delete it there too. We never receive any of these.

Subscription records

Deleting data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records linked to a random app user ID (not your name). To ask us to request deletion of the RevenueCat record, open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues titled “Tolanama subscription record deletion”. Do not post your Google Play order number, email address or any other personal details there. We will reply on the issue with a private way to send the order number (it starts with GPA.), then ask RevenueCat to delete the record, usually within 30 days.

What is deleted: all app data on the phone, immediately. What may be kept: a copy of the list in your own Google account backup until you delete it or it is replaced, any files you shared yourself, and Google Play / RevenueCat purchase records, kept under their policies for tax, accounting and fraud prevention. We hold no other data about you.
