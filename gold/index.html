Know exactly what gold and jewellery your family owns.

Weight, purity, bill, photo, who it belongs to and where it is kept — for every necklace, bangle, chain, coin and payal. In English and हिन्दी.

Get it on Google Play

Android, coming soon to Google Play. Free for up to 10 items; Tolanama Pro, an optional subscription, adds unlimited items, the inventory PDF, a full backup file and reports by family member and place. No ads, no account and no sign-up.

  • A record of every piece

    Gross, stone and net weight, 24K/22K/18K/14K, silver 999/925 or platinum 950, making charges, shop, price, and photos of the jewellery and its bill.

  • Today’s value at your rate

    Type the 24K gold rate per 10 g and silver per kg. Tolanama works out 22K, 18K and 14K, each item’s value, the total and gain or loss — and keeps your rate history.

  • For insurance, lockers and wills

    Make an inventory PDF for one family member or one bank locker, with or without photos, and share it with the people you choose.

Tolanama only keeps your own records. It never fetches gold rates from the internet, never buys, sells or stores gold, and never lends money. The gold-loan figure is a rough estimate for your information, not an offer, and the app is not linked to any lender.

Your data stays on your phone

There is no account and no Tolanama server. Your jewellery list, photos and bills are saved inside the app on your phone. If your phone’s Google backup is on, Android can also keep the list (not the photos) in your own Google account backup. Pro users can export one backup file with everything, photos included, and keep it wherever they like.
