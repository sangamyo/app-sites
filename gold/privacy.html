Privacy policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This policy explains what information the Tolanama Android app and this website (together, "the app") handle. The app is made by an independent developer, the developer named on Tolanama’s Google Play listing ("we", "us").

The short version. Tolanama has no accounts and no server of its own. Your jewellery list, weights, purity, prices, family members, places, rates and the photos of your jewellery and bills are stored on your phone. We cannot see them. If your phone’s Google backup is turned on, Android may copy the app’s list (not the photos) into your own Google account backup, which Google stores and you control in your phone’s settings. Subscription purchase data is handled by Google Play and RevenueCat. The app never goes online for gold rates, shows no ads, and we do not sell data.

1. Data stored on your phone

  • Jewellery items: name, type, metal, purity, gross weight, stone weight, making charges, purchase date, shop, price paid, notes (for example a HUID / hallmark number), the family member it belongs to and where it is kept (for example “Bank locker”).
  • Photos of the jewellery and of its bill, taken with the camera or picked by you. They are copied into the app’s private storage, not into your phone’s gallery.
  • Family members (the names you type) and the gold, silver and platinum rates you type in, with their dates.
  • Your preference for the app language.

This data is kept inside the app’s private storage on your phone. It is never uploaded to us. If you uninstall the app or use Delete all data, it is erased (see Delete your data).

Android Auto Backup (your Google account)

Tolanama keeps Android Auto Backup enabled so your register can survive a lost or replaced phone. If backup is turned on in your phone’s settings, Android may copy the app’s database (items, weights, prices, family members, places, rates and settings) into your own Google account backup, stored by Google under Google’s privacy policy and, on Android 9 and later, encrypted with your phone’s screen lock. Photos of your jewellery and bills are not included in this backup; the app excludes them. We never receive or see this backup. To turn it off, go to your phone’s Settings → Google → Backup (the exact menu names vary by phone) and switch off backup; you can also delete existing backups there.

2. Data that leaves your phone

Subscriptions (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription. RevenueCat and Google Play receive a random app user ID created on your phone (not your name, phone or email) and purchase information such as the product, price, purchase date, trial status and renewal status, plus basic device information (for example the app version, OS version and country/store). They never receive your jewellery records. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy.

Things you choose to share

When you share an inventory PDF or a photo, or export a backup file, the content goes wherever you choose to send it (for example WhatsApp, email or Google Drive), under that service’s own privacy policy. An inventory PDF and a backup file show what jewellery you own and where it is kept, and a backup file contains all your photos. They are not password-protected, so share and store them carefully.

No other network use

Apart from the subscription check, Tolanama does not use the internet. It never fetches gold or silver rates — you type them in — and has no analytics, crash reporting or advertising software. The links the app opens are Google Play’s subscriptions page and this website’s privacy policy and terms.

3. What we do not collect

  • No account, email address or password. There is no sign-in.
  • No bank logins, card numbers, UPI PINs or KYC documents. The app never buys, sells or stores gold and never moves money.
  • No access to your contacts, location, microphone, SMS or phone state, and no access to your photo gallery as a whole: photos are picked one at a time through the system photo picker, or taken with the camera when you tap Camera. Restoring a backup uses the system file picker, which only gives the app the one file you pick.
  • No advertising identifiers, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.

4. How long data is kept

Data on your phone stays until you delete it in the app, use Delete all data, or uninstall the app. A copy in your Google account backup is kept and deleted by Google according to your backup settings (see section 1). Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies (for example for tax, accounting and fraud prevention). You can ask us to request deletion of the RevenueCat record linked to your app user ID (see Delete your data).

5. Your choices and rights

You can view, edit, export and delete all of your data in the app at any time. Because we do not hold your data, we cannot view, correct or recover it for you, so please keep a backup. Depending on where you live (for example India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the EU or California), you may have additional rights under local law; contact us (see section 8) and we will help where we can.

6. Children

The app is meant for adults keeping their family’s records. It is not intended for children.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and for important changes we will tell you in the app.

8. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues and please say which app: Tolanama. Issues there are public, so please do not include personal details such as what jewellery you own, where it is kept, bills or your Google Play order number.
