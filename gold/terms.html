1. Who can use the app

2. What the app does, and does not do

  • The app is a record-keeping tool: you record your family’s jewellery, its weight, purity, bills and photos, and the gold, silver and platinum rates you choose to type in.
  • Values are estimates from your own entries. The app multiplies the net weight you enter by the rate you enter and a standard purity ratio (22K = 91.6%, 18K = 75%, 14K = 58.5% of the 24K rate; silver 925 = 92.5% of the 999 rate; platinum 950 = 95%). It counts metal only — not making charges, GST or stones. A jeweller, bank, insurer or buyer will test the jewellery and use their own rate and deductions, so the price you actually get can be different.
  • The app never fetches rates, never buys, sells or stores gold, and never collects, holds or transfers money.
  • Gold-loan estimate: the app can show “you could borrow about ₹X”, which is 75% of the value of your gold of 18K and above at your rate. It is information only, not a loan offer, not a credit assessment and not a recommendation of any lender. We do not lend money, arrange loans or work with any bank or NBFC. Lenders decide the value, the loan amount, interest and charges themselves.
  • The inventory PDF is your own record. It is not a valuation certificate, appraisal, hallmark report or proof of ownership, and we do not promise that any insurer, bank, court or other party will accept it. Nothing in the app is financial, legal, tax or investment advice.
