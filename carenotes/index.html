Every doctor’s visit, written down and shared with your siblings.

For families sharing the care of a parent. Whoever goes to the appointment writes it down once, and everyone else sees it.

Get it on Google Play

Android, coming soon to Google Play. Free for 2 visits a month per family; medications, tasks and sharing are free. One Pro subscription covers the whole family circle. No ads.

  • Visit notes

    Date, doctor, specialty, reason, notes, and the questions to ask next time. Newest first, for everyone in the circle.

  • Medications and tasks

    One shared medication list. Tasks with a due date and a person assigned, plus a reminder on that day.

  • Record on your phone

    Record the visit, after asking everyone in the room. Recordings stay on your phone and are never uploaded.

Not medical advice. CareNotes helps you organise and record. It does not diagnose, interpret results or advise on treatment. Always follow the advice of your parent’s doctors.

How it works

  1. Create a care circle for your parent. Only their first name or a nickname is needed.
  2. Your brothers and sisters join with a 6-character invite code (up to 6 people).
  3. Add visits, medications and tasks together. Share a visit as text with anyone who isn’t in the app yet.
