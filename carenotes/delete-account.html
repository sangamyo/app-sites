Delete your CareNotes account

You can delete your CareNotes account here without opening the app. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Right away: your account (email address, name and hashed password). You are removed from your care circle.
  • If you were the only member of the circle: the circle and all of its visits, medications and tasks are deleted right away too.
  • If other members are still in the circle: the shared visits, medications and tasks, including ones you added, stay available to them, because they are also their record. Your name and email are deleted with your account; items you added keep only an internal ID and show as “Former member”. They can delete them in the app or by deleting their own accounts.
  • Audio recordings: we never had them. They are only on your phone. Deleting your account in the app, signing out or leaving the circle deletes them from the phone. This web form can’t reach your phone, so sign out of the app or uninstall it to remove them.
  • Task reminders live on your phone. Deleting your account in the app, signing out or leaving the circle cancels them all on that phone. This web form can’t reach your phone, so sign out of the app or uninstall it to remove them.
  • Subscription: deleting your account does not cancel a Pro subscription you bought. Pro is per circle: it stays active for the circle until you cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Purchase records: Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies (for example for tax and fraud prevention). Contact us (see below) if you want us to request deletion of the RevenueCat record. Details are in our privacy policy.

Delete with your email and password

Can’t sign in?

This version of CareNotes has no password reset. If you are signed in on a phone, you can delete your account there: Settings tab → Account → Delete account. Otherwise open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues titled “CareNotes account deletion”. Issues there are public, so do not post your email address or any other personal details there. We will reply on the issue with a private way to confirm the account is yours, then delete it as described above, usually within 30 days.
