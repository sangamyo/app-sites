Privacy policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what information the CareNotes Android app and this website (together, "the app") collect, why, who else handles it, and how you can delete it. The app is made by an independent developer, the developer named on CareNotes’s Google Play listing ("we", "us").

The short version. Your account and your care circle’s visits, medications and tasks are stored on our server so everyone in the circle sees the same notes. Audio recordings stay on your phone and are never uploaded. AI features are off: no notes, transcripts or recordings are sent to any AI service. CareNotes is not medical advice. We do not sell your data and the app shows no ads. You can delete your account at any time, in the app or on our account deletion page.

1. What we collect

Account information

  • Your email address and the name you choose, used to sign you in and to show the other members of your circle who is in it.
  • Your password, stored only in hashed form. We cannot see your actual password.

Care circle data you enter

  • Circle: your parent’s first name or nickname (we never ask for a full name, date of birth or address), the invite code, and the names of the members.
  • Visits: date, doctor’s name, specialty, reason for the visit, your notes, any transcript you type or paste, and questions to ask next time.
  • Medications: name, dose, schedule, start and stop dates, notes, and which visit added it.
  • Tasks: title, due date, which circle member it is assigned to, and whether it is done.
  • For each record, which member created it and when it was created and last changed.

This data is stored on our server, which runs on Render and keeps its data in a MongoDB Atlas cloud database, so that everyone in the circle sees the same information on their phones.

Visit notes and medications are health information about your parent. Please only add what the family needs to coordinate care, and where possible let your parent know you keep these notes.

Audio recordings (stay on your phone)

If you record a visit, the app uses the microphone only after you tap record and go through the consent step. The recording is saved in the app’s private storage on your phone only. It is never uploaded to our server or anyone else, and other circle members cannot hear it. Deleting the visit on your phone deletes the recording. Recordings are also deleted from your phone when you delete your account, sign out or leave the circle in the app, and uninstalling the app removes them all. If the app signs you out by itself because your login expired, recordings stay on the phone so you don’t lose them when you sign back in; if someone signs in to a different care circle on that phone, recordings that don’t belong to their circle are deleted. The app turns off Android’s automatic cloud backup, so recordings are not copied to Google Drive.

AI features are off

AI summaries and AI transcription are switched off and not used in this version of CareNotes. No visit notes, transcripts, recordings or other data are sent to any AI provider, and our server makes no calls to outside AI services. If we ever offer these features, we will update this policy first and ask for your permission in the app before any of your data is used for them. Audio is never uploaded, and this policy will be updated before any audio upload is enabled.

Task reminders

Task reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your phone, at 9:00 on the due date of open tasks assigned to you or to nobody. They are built on your phone from the circle’s task list; nothing about them is sent to our server. We do not run a push notification service and do not collect a push token. The app asks for notification permission only when there is a reminder to schedule, and you can turn reminders off in Settings → Reminders. Signing out, deleting your account or leaving the circle cancels every reminder scheduled on that phone.

Purchase information

CareNotes Pro is sold and billed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your circle has Pro. RevenueCat and Google Play receive your circle’s ID (a random ID, not your name, email or your parent’s name) and purchase information such as the product, price, purchase date, trial status and renewal status, along with basic technical details such as your device’s language and currency settings and the app version. One purchase covers everyone in the circle. Pro is per circle: it stays active for the circle until the subscriber cancels it in Google Play, even if they leave the circle or delete their account. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy.

Technical data

Our server receives your IP address and basic request information when the app talks to it. We use the IP address only to keep the service secure, for example to limit repeated sign-in and account-deletion attempts; it is held in memory for about 15 minutes for this and is not saved in our database. Our server’s own log lines record only the kind of request, the address on our server it was sent to, the result and how long it took; they never contain your notes, medications, email address or password. Our hosting provider also keeps request logs for a limited time under its own policies. Your sign-in token is stored in secure storage on your phone.

What we do not collect

  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, or photos and other files. The only phone permissions the app asks for are the microphone (to record a visit, only after you tap record) and notifications (for task reminders).
  • No connection to any doctor’s office, hospital, patient portal or insurer. We do not import medical records.
  • No advertising identifiers. The app has no ads, and no analytics or crash reporting software.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep it secure.
  • To share the circle’s visits, medications and tasks between its members.
  • To check whether your circle has Pro.
  • To answer questions and requests you send us.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not use it to train AI models, and we never give medical advice.

3. Who can see or handle your data

  • Members of your care circle. Everyone who joined with the invite code (at most 6 people) can see and edit all of the circle’s visits, medications and tasks, and sees your name (not your email address).
  • People you share with. When you share a visit as text, it goes wherever you choose to send it (for example email or WhatsApp), under that service’s own privacy policy.
  • Service providers that run parts of the service for us: Render (server hosting), MongoDB Atlas (database), RevenueCat (Pro status) and Google Play (billing). They may only use the data to provide their service to us.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of users.

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for advertising.

4. Security

Data travels between the app and our server over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored hashed. Access to the database is restricted. Please use a password you do not use anywhere else, and only share the invite code with family members you trust.

5. How long we keep data

  • We keep your account and circle data for as long as your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, your account (email, name, hashed password) is deleted immediately and you are removed from the circle.
  • If other members are still in the circle, the shared visits, medications and tasks, including ones you added, stay available to them, because they are also their record. Your name and email are deleted with your account; items you added keep only an internal ID and show as “Former member”. If you were the last member, the circle and all of its visits, medications and tasks are deleted immediately.
  • Recordings exist only on your phone. Deleting your account in the app, signing out or leaving the circle deletes them from that phone. Deleting your account on this website can’t reach your phone, so sign out of the app or uninstall it to remove them.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies (for example for tax and fraud prevention). You can contact us (see section 12) to ask us to request deletion of the RevenueCat record.
  • If our database provider keeps backups, deleted data can remain in them for a limited time until they are replaced, and is not used in the meantime.

6. Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: Settings tab → Account → Delete account.
  • On the web: our account deletion page. You don’t need the app installed.
  • If you can’t sign in: see the account deletion page for how to ask us without posting personal details.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your circle’s data in the app at any time. You can also contact us (see section 12) to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it. Depending on where you live (for example California or another US state, the EU, the UK or India), you may have more rights under local law; we will honor valid requests and reply within 30 days.

8. Not medical advice

CareNotes helps families organise and record information. It does not diagnose, interpret test results or advise on treatment, and it is not a medical record system. Always follow the advice of a qualified health professional.

9. Children

The app is meant for adults caring for a parent. It is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

10. Where data is processed

Our server and database are run by our service providers (Render and MongoDB Atlas), which may store and process data in a country other than yours, including the United States. RevenueCat and Google Play also process purchase information in other countries. By using the app, you understand that your data may be handled outside the country where you live.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and for important changes we will tell you in the app.

12. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/sangamyo/app-sites/issues and say which app: CareNotes. Issues there are public, so please do not include personal details such as your email address, your parent’s name, visit notes or medications.
