CareNotes is an organising and note-taking tool. It does not diagnose, check symptoms, interpret test results or advise on treatment, and nothing in the app is medical advice. Always follow the advice of a qualified health professional. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

3. Recording visits

Before you record, ask the doctor and everyone present for permission. Some US states and other places require everyone’s consent to record a conversation. You are responsible for following the recording laws where the visit takes place. Recordings stay on your phone and are never uploaded.

4. Care circles

A care circle has up to 6 members. Anyone who joins with your invite code can see and edit all of the circle’s visits, medications and tasks. Only share the code with family members you trust. You can get a new code or leave the circle at any time. Respect your parent’s privacy and only record what the family needs to coordinate care.
